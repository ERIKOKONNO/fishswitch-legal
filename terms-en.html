4. Forecast information

The app shows how likely you are to catch fish, based on external tide and weather data and your own fishing records. What is shown may differ from actual conditions because of data delays, outages of external services, network conditions, inaccuracies in spot locations and similar causes.

  • Tide data for Japan comes from tide736.net (estimated from the phase of the moon when it cannot be retrieved).
  • Tide data for New Zealand is based on tide predictions from Toitū Te Whenua Land Information New Zealand (LINZ), processed by Sakana Switch. It is not an official tide table and must not be used for navigation.
  • Weather data is provided by Open-Meteo.
